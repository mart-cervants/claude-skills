## Rules for ideas
- Every idea comes from a real source (see `content-ideas` skill), not "give me
  10 ideas".
- One idea → one post-sized point. A strong idea may be split across formats.
- Prefer ideas the audience already asks about, in their words.

## Idea sources (ranked)
1. Questions customers/prospects actually ask
2. Problems the owner solved (abstracted; nothing traceable)
3. Common mistakes in the audience's world
4. Myths and misconceptions
5. Process transparency
6. Seasonal/business-calendar hooks
7. Adjacent accounts — for formats/topics, never copy
