# Idea formats: post, carousel, Reel

An idea is a **core point** that can take several formats. List candidates on
each idea and choose at planning time (the posting skill does that).

## What the evidence says (Facebook, 2026) — it's mixed

- Buffer (45M+ posts): images had the highest Facebook engagement rate
  (5.20%) vs video (4.84%); video drives reach, images/carousels drive
  engagement from existing followers.
  https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/
- Socialinsider: images and albums had the highest reach rate; Reels had lower
  reach but led engagement in some quarters; another Socialinsider guide ranks
  Reels low. https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/ ,
  https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook
- Sprout Social: albums and photo posts still show high engagement.
  https://sproutsocial.com/insights/facebook-stats-for-marketers/

Reading: don't assume one format wins. Test formats on the brand's own Page and
record results in the kit's `results.md`.

## Format candidates

| Format | Good for | Production cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Card (single image)** | One tip, myth, number, definition | Low | Default; reusable template |
| **Carousel / album** | Steps, checklist, comparison, mini-guide | Medium | Strong for "save-worthy" content |
| **Reel from cards** | Same idea as a short animated sequence of cards (3–6 cards, ≤ 30 s) | Medium | Facebook lets you import photos into a Reel (60 s cap); no official step-by-step found for photo-only Reels — test in the app. Page slideshows are 3–10 images, ≤ 15 s |
| **Reel, screen recording** | Demos, walkthroughs | Medium | Use fake data only (see guardrails) |
| **Reel, talking head** | Personal expertise, opinions | High | Only if the guardrails allow showing a person |
| **Text post** | Questions, opinions, short stories | Lowest | Hook in the first ~125 characters |
| **Link post** | Driving traffic | Low | Link previews; test link in text vs first comment |

## How to assign formats

- One idea → 1–3 format candidates; pick **one** per publish.
- Vary formats across the month; avoid the same format 3 times in a row.
- Plan 1–2 experiments per month (e.g. same idea as card vs Reel) to learn what
  the Page's audience prefers.
- A strong idea can be republished in another format later (weeks apart), with
  a new hook, not a copy.
