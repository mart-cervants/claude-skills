# Conversations with real customers (best raw input)

2–3 short chats (15–20 min) with people who match the brand's audience
(`brand-profile.md`): current customers, prospects, or people the owner knows.
Goal: learn their words and pains, not to sell.

Privacy: ask permission to take notes; record **patterns, not names**; don't
share identifying details; the owner decides whether to mention the brand.

Ask in the language of the conversation; adapt wording to the audience. Present
these in the brand's language (ask the user if unsure).

## Questions (open, one at a time; adapt to the brand)

1. How do you handle [the area the brand helps with] today?
2. What task or process in that area takes the most time or causes the most headaches?
3. What would you like to know or see without having to ask anyone?
4. What do your own customers ask you again and again?
5. Have you tried to fix this before (a tool, a person, an agency)? What happened?
6. What would hold you back from hiring or buying [something like the brand's offer]?
   (price, trust, time, "I don't know what to ask for")
7. How would you describe the problem in your own words? (capture phrases verbatim)

## After the chat

- Copy natural, reusable phrases into the kit's `voice.md` (vocabulary).
- Turn each pain into 1–3 ideas in `idea-backlog.md` with source
  `conversation (customer type)`.
- Don't quote or attribute to the person; generalize.
