# Scoring and vetting ideas

Score each idea 1–5 on five criteria. Total /25. Shortlist ideas ≥ 18; below 14
drop or rework unless the user insists.

| Criterion | 1 = weak | 5 = strong |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience pain** | Nice-to-know topic | The audience feels this often and says it in their own words |
| **Specificity** | Could be about any business | Has a concrete situation, tool, step, or mistake |
| **Honest angle** | Needs invented proof, stats, or clients | Works with no claims beyond what's true (or labeled *ejemplo ilustrativo*) |
| **Effort** | Needs research, a demo, or video | A card or short text is enough |
| **Pillar fit** | Doesn't fit any pillar | Clearly fits a pillar that's under-served this month |

## Vetting questions (Content Marketing Institute)

Answer before approving:
1. Do real customers care about this? (Source: conversation, repeated question,
   search demand?)
2. Has it been done to death? (Quick look: if every Page posts the same tip,
   find the specific angle only this brand can give.)
3. Would it provoke a reaction? (A question answered, a myth challenged, a
   mistake admitted.)
4. Can we add something original — a small real example, a checklist, a demo
   with fake data?

## Flags

- `needs validation` — comes from social/search signals; confirm with a real customer
  customer before relying on it.
- `sensitive` — close to something an employer or client could recognize; reshape
  or drop (see guardrails).
- `seasonal` — has a best date (holidays, season, month-end, shopping events).

## Statuses

`idea` → `approved` → `handed off` (to facebook-page-content) → `used`
(posted). Or `dropped` with a one-line reason.
