# Facebook Page best practices (researched Nov 2026)

Evidence quality is noted. Most sources are vendor or agency blogs, so treat
numbers as direction, not measured fact. Revisit when you have your own
Page Insights data — **your data beats these benchmarks.**

## What's well supported (Sprout Social 2026 guide)

- **80/20 mix:** 80% educate/entertain/inspire, 20% promote.
- **Caption:** only the first ~125 characters show before "See more". Open with
  a question, a bold statement or a surprising fact. 1–2 relevant hashtags.
  End with a clear call to action.
- **Content calendar:** plan ahead and keep a repeatable "content engine"
  instead of deciding what to post each day.
- **Respond to comments and messages** promptly; community matters.
- **Rotate formats** (text + image, short video/Reels, carousel, link) so the
  algorithm and the audience get variety.
- **Timing:** generic benchmark is Mon–Fri 9–10 am and 3–4 pm. Use Page
  Insights (and Metricool's `get_best_time_to_post`) for your own audience.
  Schedule in the brand's time zone.

## Plausible but weaker evidence (SEO/vendor blogs)

- Several SEO blogs claim Reels get 3x+ the organic reach of photos or link posts.
  Measured data is mixed (Buffer found images with the highest engagement rate;
  Socialinsider found images/albums with the highest reach rate). Plan an
  occasional Reel as an experiment, not a bet.
- Early engagement (comments in the first hour) boosts further distribution.
  Be ready to reply right after publishing.
- Sprout's example cadence is "3-2-1" per week (3 Reels, 2 feed posts,
  1 promo). That is heavy for a solo founder.

## Defaults to start from (override in the brand kit)

- **Cadence:** 2–3 posts/week (12–16/month) is realistic for one person and fits
  Metricool's Free cap of 20 scheduled posts/month with headroom. Fewer, better
  posts beat daily filler.
- **Mix per week (3 posts):** 1 educational, 1 example/demo, 1 behind-the-scenes
  or offer. Max 1 promotional post per week.
- **Formats:** image card (default), carousel, short Reels. The evidence on which
  format wins is mixed — see `content-ideas/reference/idea-formats.md` — so test
  and record results in the kit's `results.md`.
- **A new Page's organic reach is small.** Posts mostly give credibility to
  people who arrive from ads or the website; growth comes from paid reach.
- **Link in the text** is fine to start; test the link in the first comment if
  reach looks low (A/B over a month).
- **Originality:** Meta reduces distribution of unoriginal content (reposts,
  captioned or lightly edited copies). Create your own.

## Things to test and record (feed the kit's `results.md`)

- Post type vs. reach/comments/clicks (educational vs. example vs. offer).
- Posting time slots.
- Hook styles (question vs. statement vs. number).
- Image card vs. Reel.
